# Enterprise governance

Enterprise adds organizational control around the same public contracts:
SSO/SCIM, durable agent identity, RBAC, policy, provider and data
classification, residency, budgets, retention, audit, VPC/on-premise and
air-gapped deployment.

Governance decisions are authenticated, versioned, auditable, and enforced
before execution or data transfer. Enterprise services may deny or constrain a
request, but cannot weaken public safety bounds. Operational observability is
separate from product analytics and never contains prompts, source, secrets, or
customer content.
