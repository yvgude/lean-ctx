# Team workspace

A Team workspace is a bounded collaboration scope over projects, agents,
checkpoints, handoffs, and receipts. Membership and tenant identity are
authenticated; every read, write, lease, and delivery is policy-checked.

The free envelope exposes local collaboration with explicit limits. Hosted
sync, durable shared indexes, history, backups, execution scale, retention,
analytics, and support belong to Cloud/Team Scale. No workspace operation may
leak raw content through telemetry or cross-tenant caches.
