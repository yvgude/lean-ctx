The products can be evaluated for different context-processing paths. LeanCTX's
current product boundary is a local Runtime for existing coding agents; it can
provide context selection and shaping primitives before inference. It is not a
general hosted gateway, audit product, or savings platform.

## Evidence boundary

Claims about token reduction or cost need a comparable baseline and treatment,
a declared quality threshold, a stated methodology, and inspectable evidence.
A smaller payload that harms the task is not a successful outcome. Earlier
competitive figures, guarantees, and public-arena claims are historical and are
not reproduced as current LeanCTX messaging.
